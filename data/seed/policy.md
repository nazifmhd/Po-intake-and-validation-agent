# Procurement Policy (PP-007, rev. 2026-09)

Applies to every purchase order (PO) issued by any department. The PO intake agent validates incoming
POs against this policy before they are booked; anything that does not comply goes to a human reviewer.

## Auto-approval threshold and sign-off
A PO with a grand total of USD 10,000.00 or less may be booked automatically, provided every other
section of this policy is satisfied. A PO with a grand total above USD 10,000.00 requires written
approval (sign-off) from the requesting department's director before it is booked. The agent must
never auto-approve a PO above this threshold.

## Single-PO spend limit
No single PO may exceed USD 50,000.00 without CFO approval and three competitive quotes attached.
POs must not be split into several smaller POs to stay under any threshold or limit; consecutive POs
to the same vendor for the same items within 7 days are reviewed as one.

## Approved vendors
Goods and services may only be purchased from vendors in the approved-vendor registry with status
"active". POs to unregistered vendors require vendor onboarding (due diligence, tax ID verification,
bank details check) before any commitment. POs to vendors with status "blocked" must be rejected and
reported to Compliance. The vendor tax ID on a PO, when printed, must match the registry record.

## Allowed payment terms
Allowed payment terms are Net 30, Net 45 and Net 60. Prepayment, cash on delivery, "due on receipt"
and terms longer than Net 60 (e.g. Net 90) are not allowed without Finance approval. Where a vendor
has negotiated terms in the registry, the PO should use those terms.

## Pricing and catalog compliance
Catalog items must be ordered at the catalog / contract price. A unit price more than 10% above or
below the standard catalog price is a price variance that requires review and justification by
the buyer. Items not in the catalog (non-catalog purchases) require a review of the specification
and price by Procurement.

## Required fields
Every PO must state: PO number, PO date, vendor name, requested delivery date, at least one line item
(with quantity and unit price), and the grand total. A PO missing any required field is incomplete
and must be corrected before booking.

## Currency
POs are issued in USD. POs in any other currency require Finance review so that approval thresholds
are applied to the converted USD amount.

## Arithmetic integrity
Line totals must equal quantity times unit price; the subtotal must equal the sum of line totals; and
the grand total must equal subtotal plus tax plus shipping. Any discrepancy indicates a transcription
or document error and the PO must be reviewed.

## Restricted categories
Purchases of IT hardware above USD 5,000.00 in a single PO must be coordinated with the IT department
(asset tagging). Laboratory chemicals require a safety data sheet on file. These are notes for the
reviewer and do not block booking on their own.
